## 📝 Brand Guidelines

### Logo Usage
- Always use the house emoji (🏠) with gradient background
- Maintain 12px border radius for consistency
- Use gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #667eea 0%, #764ba2 100%)`

### Typography
- Brand name: "Hyperlocal Jobs" (no hyphen in display)
- Font weight: 700 (bold)
- Color: White on dark backgrounds, gradient on light backgrounds

### Color Palette
- Primary: #667eea
- Secondary: #764ba2
- Accent: #4f46e5
- Text: #1f2937
- Light: #f9fafb
